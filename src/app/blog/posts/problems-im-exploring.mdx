## 1. Passive Idea Incubation (The "Idea Vault")

**The Problem:** Creative minds often generate more business ideas than they can execute. Most of these concepts die in static 'notes apps' because the founder lacks the bandwidth to validate them while focusing on their primary venture.

**Why It Matters to Me:** I constantly have new product ideas, but I have to shelve them to focus on school and work. It feels like wasted potential to let them sit idle.

**The Opportunity:** Leveraging AI agents to act as a 'background co-founder'—autonomously researching markets, drafting business models, and validating concepts while the human creator sleeps—so that when the time is right, the idea is ready to launch.

## 2. Friction in Local Specialized Services

**The Problem:** High-quality, independent service providers (like ski tuners, mechanics, or tutors) often lack the digital infrastructure of large chains. This forces consumers to choose between the convenience of a big shop (with long wait times and high prices) or the hassle of coordinating with a skilled independent provider.

**Why It Matters to Me:** Founding Glide (my ski tuning business) exposed this gap. I realized that my customers didn't just want waxed skis; they wanted the convenience of a digital storefront combined with the quality of a local expert.

**The Opportunity:** Building "vertical-specific" booking and management platforms that give independent operators the same operational power as large franchises, democratizing access to the service economy.

## 3. The "Experience Paradox" for Aspiring Product Managers

**The Problem:** Product Management is a role that requires experience to get, but is nearly impossible to practice outside of a corporate environment. Students learn theory but lack the "sandbox" to practice real stakeholder negotiation, roadmap prioritization, and engineering hand-offs.

**Why It Matters to Me:** As a student aiming for PM roles, I've felt the gap between classroom case studies and the messy reality of my internships. There is no 'flight simulator' for product managers.

**The Opportunity:** Creating simulated, AI-driven environments where aspiring PMs can manage virtual engineering teams, handle dynamic stakeholder requests, and see the consequences of their roadmap decisions in real-time.

## 4. The Happiness Chase

**The Problem:** We focus on material things—getting into school, landing a good job, getting a raise—and we chase happiness through achievements. We don't spend enough time in experiences that actually bring fulfillment.

**Why It Matters to Me:** I don't want to waste time and get old without having accomplished meaningful things. I want to build a life that's rich in experiences, not just achievements.

**The Opportunity:** Using AI to help create more freedom and build financial independence, enabling more time for experiences that truly matter rather than just chasing the next milestone.

## 5. Ski Resort Information Overload

**The Problem:** I'm passionate about skiing, but I don't like looking at each daily report and snowfall on each website for the resorts in Utah. It's a small inconvenience that adds up when planning ski trips.

**Why It Matters to Me:** It's a small inconvenience in my life, but it's one I experience regularly during ski season. Having to check multiple websites to compare conditions is time-consuming.

**The Opportunity:** Creating a website that scrapes all the reports and puts them on one view, making it easy to see conditions across all Utah resorts at a glance. Using AI to create the app and handle the web scraping automatically.
