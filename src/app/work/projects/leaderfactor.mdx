## Overview

From January to April 2026 I was a Product Manager Intern at **LeaderFactor**, a leadership assessment SaaS company in Lehi, Utah. LeaderFactor's products help organizations measure and develop leadership skills. My work centered on a new **direct-to-consumer (D2C)** motion and on using data to strengthen the product's core skill model.

## What I did

### Prototyped and tested D2C funnels

- Prototyped direct-to-consumer funnels across **landing pages, messaging, and pricing**.
- Ran **A/B experiments** to learn which versions moved people through the funnel.

### Validated the 15-skill model with data

- Analyzed **usage and survey data** to validate LeaderFactor's **15-skill leadership model**.
- Surfaced **onboarding and retention** opportunities from what the data showed.

### Shaped positioning and go-to-market

- Built **competitor scans, personas, and customer journey maps** to inform positioning and go-to-market strategy.

### Turned research into decisions

- Produced concise **executive readouts** covering findings, KPIs, and the next experiments to run.
- Drafted **UX copy** and **lightweight PRDs** so ideas could move straight into execution.

## Skills

A/B testing · Funnel and pricing experiments · Survey and usage analysis · Competitive research · Personas and journey mapping · PRDs and executive communication
