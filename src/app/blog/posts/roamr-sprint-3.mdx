## Project Overview

Roamr is a destination discovery app built for US travelers who want to explore Europe but don't know where to start. The app guides users through a swipe-based card mechanic — marking destinations yes, no, or maybe — and builds a personalized trip board from their choices.

The goal is to make European trip planning feel inspiring rather than overwhelming, by curating a focused set of destinations and letting users build toward a trip at their own pace.

**Tech stack:** Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, deployed on Vercel.

## User Feedback Summary

Sprint 3 included a round of informal user testing with five participants — a mix of college students and recent graduates who had expressed interest in traveling to Europe.

**What users loved:** The swipe-based card mechanic for marking destinations yes/no/maybe felt intuitive and fun. Users appreciated the curated selection — having a focused set of European destinations made the decision feel manageable, not overwhelming. The personal trip board gave a clear sense of progress and building toward something real.

**What users wanted more of:** Testers asked for more information per destination card — cost estimates, best travel season, and approximate flight time from major US cities. A few users wanted filtering by trip length or budget. Navigation between the discovery flow and the trip board wasn't always obvious on first use.

**Key takeaway:** Users validated the core loop — browse, decide, save — but surfaced a gap between inspiration and actionable planning. The next sprint opportunity is bridging discovery to logistics: helping users go from "I want to go to Lisbon" to understanding what that trip actually looks like.
