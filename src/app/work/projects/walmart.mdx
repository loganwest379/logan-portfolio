## Overview

I spent the summer of 2026 as a Product Manager Intern on Walmart Global Tech's **Accelerated Pickup & Delivery (APD)** team in Bentonville, Arkansas. APD runs automated fulfillment sites that pick online grocery orders for fast pickup and delivery. My work focused on helping the team understand why performance varied from site to site, and what to do about it.

## The problem

The team had 19 live sites and no single, reliable view of how each one was performing. Questions went to analysts as one-off requests, which made it slow to spot problems and hard to agree on where Product and Engineering should focus next.

## What I did

### Built a self-service performance dashboard

- Built a **Python/FastAPI + BigQuery** dashboard that gave the team real-time performance visibility across all **19 fulfillment sites**.
- Replaced one-off analyst requests with a tool the team could use on its own.
- Transferred full ownership to **2 full-time PMs** so it would keep running after my internship.

### Diagnosed and closed a product-selection gap

- Designed a diagnostic framework to separate the reasons orders weren't fully completing, and isolated a **single fixable product-selection gap** at one site.
- Worked with the team to close it, adding **1,463 products** to that site's assortment.
- Result: order completion at that site rose by **3.6 percentage points**.

### Prioritized where to focus

- Developed a **site-complexity model** that ranked all 19 locations, so Product and Engineering could prioritize the sites where effort would matter most.
- Presented the findings to **SVP-level leadership across 4 review rounds**, aligning **10+ cross-functional stakeholders** on next steps.

## Results

- **19 sites** with real-time performance visibility through a self-service tool
- **+3.6 points** of order completion at one site after adding **1,463 products**
- A ranked view of all sites, reviewed with SVP-level leadership
- Dashboard ownership handed to **2 full-time PMs**
