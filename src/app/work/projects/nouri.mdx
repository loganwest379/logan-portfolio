## Overview

From January to April 2025 I was a Product Manager Intern at **Nouri.ai**, a CRM and events platform in Provo, Utah. It was my first PM internship, and I focused on helping new users get value from the product faster and on finding where client workflows were slowing down.

## What I did

### Made onboarding clearer

- Created **targeted onboarding videos and content** for the parts of the product where new users got stuck.
- Result: user confusion dropped by **about 30%**, improving product adoption and retention.

### Found where workflows broke down

- Analyzed **client workflows and processes** end to end.
- Surfaced **10+ inefficiencies** and delivered recommendations to **senior leadership**.

### Improved the website experience

- Wrote a report on **navigation and feature optimization** for the website.
- The recommendations outlined a **25% efficiency gain** in how users moved through the site.

Onboarding design · User research · Process and workflow analysis · Executive communication
